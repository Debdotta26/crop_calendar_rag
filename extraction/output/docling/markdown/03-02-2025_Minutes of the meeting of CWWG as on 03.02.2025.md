## Summary Points of the CWWG meeting:

## I. Preliminary Report of area coverage under Rabi season

## Rabi crops:

-  As on 31.01.2025 area sown under Rabi crops is 661.03 lakh ha as compared to 651.42 lakh ha during the corresponding period of last year.
-  Sowing  Status  of  Wheat:  As  on  31.01.2025,  area  of  324.88  lakh  ha  has  been  sown under  wheat  this  year  against  the  previous  year  area  of  318.33  lakh  ha  while  the normal area of wheat is 312.35 lakh ha.
-  Sowing status of Rapeseed &amp; Mustard: As on 31.01.2025, an area of 89.30 lakh ha area has been sown under mustard against the previous year area of 91.83 lakh ha while the normal area under Mustard is 79.16 lakh ha.
-  Sowing status of Jowar: As on 31.01.2025, the area covered under Rabi Jowar is 24.35 lakh. Sowing is almost completed for the current season.

## Rabi Horticulture Crops :

-  For  Onion,  Potato  and  Tomato,  an  area  sown  has  been  reported  by  the  major producing States, 9.62 lakh ha, 19.81 lakh ha, 2.15 lakh ha as against total sown area of 8.01 lakh ha, 19.49 lakh ha, 2.35 lakh ha, respectively in the last year.

## II. Weather:

-  Isolated  light  rainfall/snowfall  is  likely  over  the  Western  Himalayan  Region  on  3rd February 2025, with scattered to  fairly widespread light  to  moderate  rainfall/snowfall expected on 4th and  5th  February  under the influence of the  second  Western Disturbance  (WD).  A  fresh  WD  is  likely  to  affect  Northwest  India  from  8th  February, bringing  isolated  to  scattered  light  to  moderate  rainfall/snowfall  over  the  Western Himalayan Region on 8th and 9th February.
-  A gradual rise in minimum temperatures by 2-3°C is likely over the plains of Northwest India  during the next  3-4  days and a  similar  rise  is  expected over  Maharashtra in  the next 2 days, with no significant change thereafter.
-  No  significant  change  in  minimum  temperatures  is  expected  over  West  and  Central India  during  the  next  24  hours,  with  a  2-3°C  fall  likely  over  these  regions  in  the following 3 days.
-  Overall, rainfall is likely to be above normal over south Peninsular India and normal to above normal over northeast India during the week.

## III. Rainfall distribution:

-  All  India  seasonal  cumulative  (01 st   Jan  2025    -  03 rd   Feb  2025)  rainfall  in  %  departure from its Long Period Average (LPA) was (-)70%, with East &amp; North East-India (-)49%, North-West India (-)79%,  Central India (-)97% and South Peninsula (-)10%.

## IV. Water Reservoir Status:

-  As on 30th January, 2025, the total live water storage in 155 important reservoirs across the country (monitored by CWC) is 114.914 Billion Cubic Meter, which is 64% of total live storage capacity (180.85 BCM).
-  The current year's storage is nearly 121 percent of last year's storage and 116 percent of the average storage of the last ten years during the corresponding period.
-  The overall storage position is better than the corresponding period of last year in the country as a whole and is also better than the Normal storage during the corresponding period.

## V. Groundwater Levels Scenario

-  About 66.6% of the total monitoring wells show depth to water level in the range of 0 to 5 m below ground level and 29.1% show depth to water level in the range of 5 to 10 m below ground level. As per decadal water level fluctuation with a mean (August 2014 to 2023) and August 2024 analysis, 64.9% of monitoring wells (8199) show a rise whereas 35.1% (4435) show a fall in August month.

## VI. Seeds:

-  For  Rabi  2024-25,  availability  of  certified/  quality  seed  in  the  country  is  337.98  lakh quintals against the requirement of 307.07 lakh quintals indicating an overall surplus of 30.90 lakh quintals of seeds.

## VII. Fertilizers:

-  Fertilizers position is stated to be comfortable in all products.

## VIII. Pests &amp; Diseases:

Over all intensity of pests and disease remained below Economic Threshold Level (ETL) and no major pest incidence was recorded.

Details of each sector have been enclosed at Annex.

## Rainfall Forecast:

## 30 th  Jan - 05 th  Feb 2025

-  Two  fresh Western  Disturbances are likely to affect Northwest India between 01st  to  03rd  February,  2025.  Under  the influence of these systems.
-  Scattered  to  fairly  widespread  light  to moderate and isolated to scattered rainfall/snowfall activity likely over over Himachal Pradesh during 30th January05th February, Uttarakhand during 01 st  February-05th February and isolated  to  scattered  light  to  moderate rainfall  over  Punjab,  Haryana  during 31st January05th February, Uttar Pradesh,  Rajasthan  during  03rd  -05th February.
-  Overall,  rainfall  is  likely  to  be  above normal over south Peninsular India and normal to above normal over northeast India during the week.

## 06 th  Feb - 12 th  Feb 2025

-  No  active  western  disturbance  is  likely to influence northwest India during the week.  However,  due  to  feeble  Western disturbances, light/moderate isolated to scattered  rainfall/snowfall  likely  over Western Himalayan Region during some/many days of the week.
-  Light/moderate isolated to scattered rainfall is also likely over northeast India during some days of the week.
-  Under  the  influence  of  easterly  winds from Bay of Bengal, isolated to scattered rainfall  likely  over  South  Peninsular  &amp; adjoining central India during some days of the week.
-  Overall, rainfall is likely to be normal to above normal over parts of central India and  northeastern  states;  below  normal over rest parts of the country during the week.

## Temperature Forecast:

## 30 th  Jan - 05 th  Feb 2025

-  Gradual rise in minimum temperatures by 2-3°C likely over the  Northwest,  East,  Central  and West  India  during  1st  half  of  the week  and  no  significant  change thereafter.
-  No significant change in minimum temperatures likely over rest parts of the country.

## 06 th  Feb - 12 th  Feb 2025

-  Minimum temperatures are likely to be between 4-8°C over Punjab, Haryana,  Chandigarh  &amp;  Delhi  and north Rajasthan and above 8°C over rest parts of the country during the week.
-  Minimum temperatures are likely to be  near  normal  or  slightly  below normal by 1-2°C over most parts of the country during the week.
-  There is no probability of cold wave conditions over any part of the country and there is less probability of  significant  dense  fog  over  any part of the country.
