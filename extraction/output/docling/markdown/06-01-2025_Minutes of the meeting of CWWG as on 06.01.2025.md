## Summary Points of the CWWG meeting:

## I. Preliminary Report of area coverage under Rabi season

## Rabi crops:

-  As  on  January  03,  2025,  the  area  sown  under  Rabi  crops  is  622.76  lakh  ha  as compared to 621.52 lakh ha during the corresponding period of last year.  Of this, area of 319.74 lakh ha has been sown under wheat this year against the previous year area of 314.25 lakh ha (increase of 5.49 lakh ha).

## Rabi Horticulture Crops :

-  For  Onion,  Potato  and  Tomato,  an  area  sown  has  been  reported  by  the  major producing States, 7.02 lakh ha, 17.14 lakh ha, 1.65 lakh ha as against total sown area of 5.78 lakh ha, 11.80 lakh ha, 1.30 lakh ha, respectively in the last year.

## II. Weather:

-  Light  to  moderate  scattered/fairly  widespread  rainfall/snowfall  likely  over Western Himalayan Region and isolated to scattered rainfall over adjoining plains of northwest India during 1st half of the week.
-  Isolated  to  scattered  rainfall  likely  over  South  Peninsular  &amp;  adjoining  central India during some days of the week.
-  Dense fog conditions during late night to early morning hours likely to continue over parts of North India till 09th January.
-  No significant change in minimum temperatures likely over northwest, Central &amp; West India during next 3-4 days.
-  There is a low to moderate probability of cold wave conditions at isolated places over  Punjab,  Haryana,  adjoining  north  Rajasthan  &amp;  Himachal  Pradesh  during some days of the week.
-  Overall, rainfall is likely to be above normal over most parts of Central &amp; South Peninsular India; normal to above normal over many parts of plains of northwest India; below normal over rest parts of the country during the week.

## III. Rainfall distribution:

-  All India seasonal cumulative (01 st  Jan  - 6 th  Jan 2025) rainfall in % departure from its Long Period Average (LPA) was (-)76%, with East &amp; North East-India (-)99%, NorthWest India (-)59%,  Central India (-)100% and South Peninsula (-)76%.

## IV. Water Reservoir Status:

-  As  on  02nd  January,  2025,  the  total  live  water  storage  in  155  important  reservoirs across the country (monitored by CWC) is 132.293 Billion Cubic Meter, which is 73% of total live storage capacity (180.85 BCM).
-  The current year's storage is nearly 125 percent of last year's storage and 119 percent of the average storage of the last ten years during the corresponding period.
-  The overall storage position is better than the corresponding period of last year in the country as a whole  and is also better than the  Normal  storage during the corresponding period.

## V. Groundwater Levels Scenario

-  About 66.6% of the total monitoring wells show depth to water level in the range of 0 to 5 m below ground level and 29.1% show depth to water level in the range of 5 to 10 m below ground level. As per decadal water level fluctuation with a mean (August 2014 to 2023) and August 2024 analysis, 64.9% of monitoring wells (8199) show a rise whereas 35.1% (4435) show a fall in August month.

## VI. Seeds:

-  For  Rabi  2024-25,  availability  of  certified/  quality  seed  in  the  country  is  337.98  lakh quintals against the requirement of 307.07 lakh quintals indicating an overall surplus of 30.90 lakh quintals of seeds.

## VII. Fertilizers:

-  Fertilizers position is stated to be comfortable in all products.

## VIII. Pests &amp; Diseases:

-  Intensity of pests and diseases, at some fields, has been reported to be above Economic Threshold Level for Maize (FAW) in Tamil Nadu. The advisory has been issued through NPSS  and  regular  monitoring  is  being  carried  out.  The  overall  intensity  of  pests  and diseases remained below Economic Threshold Level.

## Rainfall Forecast:

## 2 nd  Jan - 08 th  Jan 2025

-  Light to moderate rainfall at  many places likely over Northeastern states on 07th &amp; 08th January.
-  A fresh Western Disturbance is likely to affect Northwest India from 10-12th January.  Under  its  influence,  Light  to moderate  rainfall/snowfall  likely  over Western  Himalayan  region  and  light rainfall  over  the  plains  of  Northwest India during the same period.

## 09 th  Jan - 15 th  Jan 2025

-  Under  the  influences  of  active  western disturbance, light to moderate scattered/fairly widespread rainfall/snowfall likely over Western Himalayan Region and isolated to scattered  rainfall  over  adjoining  plains of northwest India during 1st half of the week.
-  Under the influence of likely  formation of  cyclonic  circulation/  Low  Pressure area, isolated to scattered rainfall likely over South Peninsular &amp; adjoining central  India  during  some  days  of  the week.
-  Overall,  rainfall  is  likely  to  be  above normal  over  most  parts  of  Central  &amp; South Peninsular India; normal to above normal  over  many  parts  of  plains  of northwest India; below normal over rest parts of the country during the week.

## Temperature Forecast:

## 2 nd  Jan - 08 th  Jan 2025

-  Minimum temperatures are expected to gradually rise by 2-3°C over  Northwest  India  during  the next  five  days.  Over  Central  and East  India,  no  significant  change  is expected  for  the  next  three  days, followed by a gradual increase of 23°C.  In  the  Maharashtra  region,  a fall  of  2-3°C  is  likely  over  the  next two days, with no significant change thereafter. Gujarat is expected to experience no significant change in minimum temperatures over the next five days.
-  Dense fog conditions to prevail during  night/early  morning  hours in some parts of Punjab &amp; Haryana Chandigarh during 06th-08th; in isolated pockets of Punjab &amp; Haryana, Chandigarh on 09th; Uttar Pradesh  &amp;  Rajasthan  during  06th  08th, West Bengal &amp; Sikkim, Jharkhand  on  06th;  Bihar  on  06th; Assam  &amp;  Meghalaya  &amp;  Nagaland, Manipur,  Mizoram  &amp;  Tripura  on 06th January .

## 09 th  Jan - 15 th  Jan 2025

-  Minimum temperatures are likely to be  between  4-8°C  over  many  parts of  north  India  (excluding  Western Himalayan  Region)  and  above  8°C over rest parts of the country during the week.

-  Minimum temperatures are likely to be  near  normal  or  slightly  above normal over most parts of northwest &amp; adjoining central India and  northeast  India.  It  is  likely  to below normal by 1-3°C over Peninsular  &amp;  adjoining  east  India during the week.
-  There is a low to moderate probability of cold wave conditions at isolated places over Punjab, Haryana, adjoining north Rajasthan &amp;  Himachal  Pradesh  during  some days of the week.
