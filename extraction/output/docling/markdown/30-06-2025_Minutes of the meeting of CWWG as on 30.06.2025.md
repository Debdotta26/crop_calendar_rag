- a) Temperature: Maximum temperatures are likely to be below normal by 2-4°C over most parts of the country except northeast India, where it is likely to be above normal by 2- 3°C.
- b) Rainfall Distribution: From June 01- 29 June, 2025, the All-India cumulative rainfall is 9% higher than its Long Period Average (LPA), with regional variations: (i) East &amp; Northeast India:(-)17%;  (ii)  Northwest  India:  42%;  (iii)  Central  India:  25%;  and  South  Peninsula:(-) 3%.

Heavy to  very  heavy  rainfall  activity  likely  to  continue  over  many  parts  of  Northwest, Central, East &amp; Northeast India during next 7 days, with isolated extremely heavy falls over Jharkhand on 29th &amp; 30th and Odisha on 29th June.

Scattered to fairly widespread rainfall very likely over central India, ghat area of western coast, eastern India &amp; northwest India.

Isolated to scattered rainfall very likely over the south peninsula India &amp; western India.

Rainfall along with thunderstorm &amp; gusty wind likely over northwest India till next 3-4 days.

Overall, above normal rainfall along with central and east and northwest India and; below normal over southern peninsula and northeast parts of India during the week.
