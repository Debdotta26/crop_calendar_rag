Key Insights

1. Summer Sowing Status: Summer sowing data collection has been started from 7th February, 2025 and the sowing is in preliminary stage.

2. Rabi Season Area Coverage :

- o As of January 31, 2025, the total area sown under Rabi crops is 661.03 lakh ha, an increase from 651.42 lakh ha last year. Wheat sowing has reached 324.38 lakh ha, surpassing both last year's 318.33 lakh ha and the normal 312.35 lakh ha.
- o For Rabi horticulture crops , major producing states report the following sowing areas: Onion 9.67 lakh ha (8.40 lakh ha last year), Potato - 19.81 lakh ha (19.50 lakh ha last year), Tomato 2.18 lakh ha (2.39 lakh ha last year).

3. Weather Update :

- a) Temperature: Minimum temperatures are likely to fall 2-3°C over Northwest, central & East India during next 3 days and no significant change thereafter. Maximum temperatures are likely above normal  by  2-3℃  over  south  peninsular  India  during  next  4-5  days  and  no  significant  change  in minimum and maximum temperatures likely over rest parts of the country during next 5 days. Overall, rainfall is likely to be below normal over all the homogeneous regions of the country during the week.
- b) Rainfall Distribution: From January 01- February 09, 2025, the All-India cumulative rainfall is 71% below its Long Period Average (LPA), with regional variations: (i) East & Northeast India: -53%; (ii) Northwest India: -76%; (iii) Central India: -97%; and South Peninsula:-24%
3. o Isolated  to  scattered  light  rainfall/snowfall  activity  likely  over  Arunachal  Pradesh  during 10th-15th increasing to fairly widespread to  widespread rainfall accompanied  with thunderstorm & lightning during 11th-13th February with isolated heavy rainfall on 11th & 12th February.
4. o Isolated to scattered light rainfall/snowfall activity likely over Sub-Himalayan West Bengal & Sikkim during 10th-14th; northeast Assam during 10th-14th; Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram & Tripura during 10th-12th February.

4. Inputs Situation

- a) Water Reservoir Status : As of February 06, 2025, the live storage in 155 major reservoirs is 110.204 BCM (61% of total capacity). Storage is at 123% of  last year's  level and 119 % of the 10-year average.  The  overall  storage  position  is  better  than  the  corresponding  period  of  last  year  in  the country as a whole and is also better than the Normal storage during the corresponding period.
- b) Groundwater Levels : Approximately 66.6% of monitoring wells show water levels between 0-5 m, while 29.1% report levels between 5-10 m. Decadal analysis (2014-2023) indicates a rise in 64.9% of wells and a decline in 35.1% as of August 2024.
- c) Seeds and Fertilizers : For Rabi 2024-25, certified seed availability is 337.98 lakh quintals against a requirement of 307.07 lakh quintals, ensuring a surplus of 30.90 lakh quintals. The fertilizer supply remains stable across all products.
5. Pests & Diseases: Pest and disease intensity remains below Economic Threshold Levels (ETL), except for Chilli (Black Thrips: Thrips parvispinus) in Andhra Pradesh, where advisories have been issued, and monitoring is ongoing.
