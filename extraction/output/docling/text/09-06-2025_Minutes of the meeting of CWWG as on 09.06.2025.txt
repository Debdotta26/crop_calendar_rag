Key Insights

1. Kharif Sowing Status: Kharif sowing data collection has started from 06 th  June, 2025 and the sowing is in preliminary stage. As on June 06 2025, the area sown under Kharif crops is 74.30 lakh ha (including 53.39 lakh ha (102% of normal area) in Sugarcane) as compared to 75.11 lakh ha during the corresponding period of last year.

2. Weather Update :

- a) Temperature: Maximum Temperatures likely  to  rise  by  2-3°C  over  Northwest  &  Central India  during  next  3  days  and  no  significant  change  thereafter.  No  significant  change  in maximum temperatures likely over rest parts of the country.
- b) Rainfall Distribution: From June 01- 09 June, 2025, the All-India cumulative rainfall is 29% lower than its Long Period Average (LPA), with regional variations: (i) East & Northeast India: (-)7%; (ii) Northwest India: (-)8%; (iii) Central India: (-)48%; and South Peninsula: (-) 54%.

An  upper  air  cyclonic  circulation  lay  over  Haryana  &  neighborhood  and  another  over Southeast Himachal Pradesh & adjoining Uttarakhand in lower tropospheric levels.

A Western Disturbance lay as a cyclonic circulation over Ladakh & adjoining Kashmir in middle tropospheric levels.

Another Western Disturbance lay as a trough in middle tropospheric westerlies roughly along Long. 54°E to the north of Lat. 25°N.

Increase  in  rainfall  activity  with  Isolated  heavy  falls  very  likely  over  south  peninsular India, west coast and northeastern states from 10th June and heavy to very heavy rainfall over Karnataka and Kerala from 13 th  June.

Light/moderate rainfall  at  isolated  places  accompanied  with  thunderstorm,  lightning  & gusty winds likely over most parts of the country.

Inputs Situation
