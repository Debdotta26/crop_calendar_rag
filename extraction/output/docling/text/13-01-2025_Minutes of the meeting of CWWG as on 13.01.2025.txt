Summary Points of the CWWG meeting:

I. Preliminary Report of area coverage under Rabi season

Rabi crops:

- As  on  January  10,  2025,  the  area  sown  under  Rabi  crops  is  632.27  lakh  ha  as compared to 631.44 lakh ha during the corresponding period of last year.  Of this, area of 320.00 lakh ha has been sown under wheat this year against the previous year area of 315.63 lakh ha (increase of 4.37 lakh ha).

Rabi Horticulture Crops :

- For  Onion,  Potato  and  Tomato,  an  area  sown  has  been  reported  by  the  major producing States, 7.04 lakh ha, 17.22 lakh ha, 1.74 lakh ha as against total sown area of 6.53 lakh ha, 12.0 lakh ha, 1.51 lakh ha, respectively in the last year.

II. Weather:

- A fresh western disturbance is likely to affect northwest India from the night of 14th  January,  2025.  Under  its  influence,  isolated  to  scattered  rainfall/snowfall activity likely over Jammu-Kashmir-Ladakh-Gilgit-Baltistan-Muzaffarabad, Himachal  Pradesh,  Uttarakhand  and  Isolated  rainfall  activity  over  Punjab, Haryana Chandigarh & Delhi, Uttar Pradesh and East &b West Rajasthan during some days of the week.
- Dense  to  very  dense  fog  conditions  likely  to  continue  over  Northwest  India during next 2- 3 days.
- Rise  in  minimum temperatures by 2-4 ℃ likely  over  Northwest  India  &  Central India during 1st half of the week and gradual fall by 2-3 ℃ thereafter.
- Overall, rainfall is likely to be above normal over most parts of south Peninsular &  adjoining  central  India  and  many  parts  of  plains  of  northwest  India;  below normal over rest parts of the country during the week.

III. Rainfall distribution:

- All India seasonal cumulative (01 st  Jan -13 th  Jan 2025) rainfall in % departure from its Long Period Average (LPA) was (-)62%, with East & North East-India (-)22%, NorthWest India (-)63%,  Central India (-)93% and South Peninsula (-)62%.

IV. Water Reservoir Status:

- As on 09th January, 2025, the total live water storage in 155 important reservoirs across the country (monitored by CWC) is 127.921 Billion Cubic Meter, which is 71% of total live storage capacity (180.85 BCM).
- The current year's storage is nearly 124 percent of last year's storage and 121 percent of the average storage of the last ten years during the corresponding period.
- The overall storage position is better than the corresponding period of last year in the country as a whole and is also better than the Normal storage during the corresponding period.

V. Groundwater Levels Scenario

- About 66.6% of the total monitoring wells show depth to water level in the range of 0 to 5 m below ground level and 29.1% show depth to water level in the range of 5 to 10 m below ground level. As per decadal water level fluctuation with a mean (August 2014 to 2023) and August 2024 analysis, 64.9% of monitoring wells (8199) show a rise whereas 35.1% (4435) show a fall in August month.

VI. Seeds:

- For  Rabi  2024-25,  availability  of  certified/  quality  seed  in  the  country  is  337.98  lakh quintals against the requirement of 307.07 lakh quintals indicating an overall surplus of 30.90 lakh quintals of seeds.

VII. Fertilizers:

- Fertilizers position is stated to be comfortable in all products.

VIII. Pests & Diseases:

Intensity of pests and diseases, at some fields, has been reported to be above Economic Threshold Level for Coconut (Black headed caterpillar) in Tamil Nadu. The Advisory has been issued by the survey team to the farms and regular monitoring is being carried out.  The  overall  intensity  of  pests  and  diseases  remained  below  Economic  Threshold Level.

Details of each sector have been enclosed at Annex.

CROP SOWING STATUS FOR RABI SEASON

Rabi Crops

- As on 10.01.2025 area sown under Rabi crops is 632.27 lakh ha as compared to 631.44 lakh ha during the corresponding period of last year.
- o Sowing Status of Wheat : As on 10.01.2025, area of 320.00 lakh ha has been sown under wheat this year against the previous year area of 315.63 lakh ha while the normal area of wheat is 312.35 lakh ha.
- o Sowing status of Rapeseed & Mustard: As on 10.01.2025, an area of 88.50 lakh ha area has been sown under mustard against the previous year area of 93.73 lakh ha while the normal area under Mustard is 79.16 lakh ha.
- o Sowing status of Jowar: As on 10.01.2025, the area covered under Rabi Jowar is 23.58 lakh. Sowing is almost completed for the current season, it is projected that around 3-4 lakh ha less area will be covered compared to previous year final achievement of 27.36 lakh  ha.  This  reduction  is  mainly  from  Maharashtra,  where  it  has  been  diverted  to maize, vegetables & wheat and from Karnataka, where part of the rabi jowar area has been shifted to gram and wheat cultivation.
- Rabi sowing for all crops is above normal except gram, as the sowing window is still open, it is expected to achieve normal area.
