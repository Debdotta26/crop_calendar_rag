Key Insights

1. Summer Sowing Status: Summer sowing data collection has been started from 7th February, 2025 and the sowing is in preliminary stage. As on February 27, 2025, the area sown under summer crops is 32.55 lakh ha as compared to 26.55 lakh ha during the corresponding period of last year. Of this, area of 25.06 lakh ha has been sown under rice this year against the previous year area of 22.24 lakh ha (increase of 2.82 lakh ha).
2. Rabi harvesting Status: As on 27.02.2025, 11.50 % of area has been harvested all over India. Approximately 19.61% area of Pulses mainly in Lathyrus (71%), Uradbean (65%), Moongbean (46%); 24.72% area of Oilseeds majorly in Rapeseed &amp; Mustard (12%); and 18.60% area of Rice has been harvested for Rabi crops 2024-25.
3. Rabi Season Area Coverage : For Rabi horticulture crops ,  major producing states report the following sowing areas: Onion - 10.31 lakh ha (9.05 lakh ha last year), Potato - 19.82 lakh ha (19.56 lakh ha last year), Tomato - 2.41 lakh ha (2.50 lakh ha last year).
